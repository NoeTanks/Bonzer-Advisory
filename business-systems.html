Before you sign up

A few useful answers.

Am I buying software or consulting?

The core purchase is access to your selected software module through Bonzer, with basic product support. Standard configuration is a separate one-time setup. Extra training, custom integrations, ongoing management and business advice are optional services.

Why buy through Bonzer instead of directly from the vendor?

You get a defined setup for your business and one advisory relationship for selecting, configuring and supporting the tools. If you already use one of these platforms, we can scope implementation or review work without making you buy another copy.

Are these ready-to-activate offers?

We begin with a fit check and written proposal. Before accepting payment, Bonzer confirms the required licence, supplier eligibility, feature entitlements, final price, activation date and cancellation terms. Workspace is subject to SuiteDash partner approval. Software is provisioned after your agreed order and payment; this website does not start a subscription.

Is the demo showing the actual software?

The Follow-up tour uses captured screens from our configured HighLevel test account. We tested the enquiry capture and booking handover with fictional data. It is a screenshot tour, not a live software session; the demo still retains the platform’s default logo. Visibility and Workspace currently use official vendor walkthroughs. Your proposal specifies your enabled features, configuration and connection scope.

Can I buy one product or bring my existing systems?

Yes. Buy any standalone module or combine them. We first check whether your existing CRM, portal or reports already solve the problem and test the specific connections needed.

Will everything sync automatically?

The HighLevel-to-AgencyAnalytics reporting connector is documented, but still needs setup and validation. A SuiteDash handover is a separate integration project if automation is required. We agree the data, direction, frequency and exception handling rather than promising universal syncing.
