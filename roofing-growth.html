Where roofing growth usually leaks

More leads can still create less profit.

The first job is not to buy more traffic. It is to decide which work is worth acquiring and build a clean path from demand to a profitable booking.

01

Repair and replacement get mixed together

A campaign can look busy while attracting low-value calls that crowd out the jobs you actually want.

02

Shared leads turn into a race to the bottom

Speed and price become the only differentiators when several roofers receive the same enquiry.

03

Missed calls disappear from the report

Paid demand has no value if the phone is not answered, the estimate is delayed or follow-up stops too early.

04

Revenue is mistaken for return

Without service, source and margin data, it is impossible to know whether the work generated enough gross profit to justify the spend.

Illustrative roofing economics

Start with the job. Work backwards.

Your numbers will be different. The audit replaces assumptions with your service mix, close rate, margin, capacity and market.

Roofing marketing questions

Straight answers, before you spend.

What kind of roofing work can the pilot target?

The first test can focus on residential replacement, flat roofing, commercial work, emergency repair or another service with strong economics. Bonzer recommends one target after reviewing margin, demand, territory and capacity.

Why use Google Search for roofing lead generation?

Search reaches people already looking for a roofer or a specific roofing service. That makes the first acquisition test more controllable and easier to connect to calls, estimates and booked work.

Do you sell shared roofing leads?

No. The campaign, landing page and tracking sit around your business and your advertising account. Bonzer does not resell the same enquiry to competing roofers.

Do you guarantee a number of roofing leads?

No. Demand, competition, website response, sales follow-up and seasonality all affect results. The pilot makes the assumptions explicit, limits the first test and measures whether the economics justify continued spending.

What numbers do you need for a free roofing growth audit?

For one target service: average job value, direct costs or gross margin, recent estimate volume, close rate if available, service area, crew capacity and current advertising performance.

Can Bonzer work with roofing companies outside Toronto?

Yes. The work is delivered remotely and priced in USD. Bonzer can support roofing contractors in Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom and Australia, subject to market fit and available capacity.
