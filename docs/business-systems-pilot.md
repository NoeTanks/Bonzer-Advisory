# Business systems pilot

Adds business-systems.html as a proposed software catalogue. The current homepage, live domain configuration and existing service pages remain unchanged. Keep noindex until the offering is ready.

## Product decisions

- Bonzer Follow-up: HighLevel branded desktop app. Standard setup: one pipeline, one enquiry form, one calendar and up to three agreed reminder workflows. Proposed retail 399/month, 990 setup in either AUD or CAD as separate price tests.
- Bonzer Workspace: SuiteDash branded client portal. One portal design, intake form, proposal template and onboarding workflow. Proposed retail 149/month, 690 setup. Confirm a SU1TE child tier supporting the scope; primary retail Pinnacle account and partner application are launch dependencies.
- Bonzer Visibility: AgencyAnalytics branded reporting portal. One dashboard, up to three supported connections and one monthly report template. Proposed retail 149/month, 490 setup. Confirm flexible monthly vendor billing and any minimum client commitment.
- Optional Finance Reports: Fathom branded reports, not a fully white-labelled application. From 199/month; setup depends on accounting data quality and scope. Confirm commercial delivery permissions before sale.

Subscriptions include module access and basic product support. One-time setup covers the defined configuration, testing and a 30-minute handover. Custom integrations, extra training, ongoing management and advisory are separately scoped.

## Before customer sales

1. Confirm vendor branding/resale entitlements, Australian/Canadian messaging support, minimum spend, usage charges, cancellation/export terms and support boundaries.
2. Confirm each retail scope and price, including the SuiteDash tier and AgencyAnalytics billing term. Do not describe partner approval as completed.
3. Obtain funded customer commitments and agree activation timing before purchasing reseller subscriptions. HighLevel fixed agency overhead needs adequate customer coverage; do not treat the first 399 subscription as a sustainable margin against a 297 USD platform bill.
4. Create a legitimate branded demo workspace when timing permits, with trial expiry and billing handled deliberately. Public recorded tours do not require paid vendor subscriptions.
5. Complete email delivery and test Calendly availability/confirmations and customer time zones.
6. Review customer terms/privacy for software delivery and third-party video players. No advertising pixel, form backend, payment collection or provisioning is added.
7. Complete mobile/desktop visual and native video playback acceptance before merging or promoting. Remove launch preview language/noindex only when ready and separately choose homepage replacement versus a new landing-page link.
