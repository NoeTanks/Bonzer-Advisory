Common questions

What you probably want to know

What size business is this right for?
Bonzer Advisory works best with businesses between roughly 10 and 200 employees that have grown past their bookkeeper or part-time accountant but are not yet at the scale for a full-time CFO. Revenue is less important than financial complexity — if your reporting, controls, or cash flow visibility are creating risk or limiting decisions, this is likely a good fit.
How does the first 30 days work?
Following the discovery consultation, a scope and fee are agreed in writing. Work begins immediately on one clearly defined problem. A documented output is delivered at the end of 30 days — whether that is a rebuilt reporting pack, a controls remediation plan, a technical accounting memo, or a finance function roadmap. No surprises on scope or cost.
Do I have to commit to a retainer upfront?
No. The 30-day fix engagement stands on its own. Many clients find that after the initial engagement they want ongoing support, at which point a retainer is discussed. But there is no obligation to continue, and the initial engagement is priced and scoped independently.
How is this different from hiring an accountant or bookkeeper?
A bookkeeper records transactions. An accountant prepares tax returns. A fractional CFO owns the financial strategy, the reporting architecture, and the decisions that flow from them. Bonzer Advisory operates at CFO and controller level — working with your leadership team, your investors, your auditors, and your lenders.
Is this delivered remotely?
Yes. All engagements are delivered remotely as standard. Bonzer Advisory serves clients across North America, Australia, the United Kingdom, and internationally. On-site availability can be arranged for specific engagements where required.
What industries do you work in?
Direct experience spans AI and technology, financial services, private equity, mining and resources, energy, media, telecommunications, real estate, and retail. The core disciplines — reporting, controls, technical accounting, and CFO leadership — are applicable across industries, and the discovery consultation will confirm fit.
