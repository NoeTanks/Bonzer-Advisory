Honest about fit

We're honest about fit

Bonzer Advisory works best with a specific kind of business. Being honest about that saves everyone time.

Bonzer is a great fit if you:

  • Run a business with annual revenue between $1M and $20M
  • Have 5 or more employees and are growing
  • Already have a bookkeeper or accountant
  • Are ready to take the finance side of your business more seriously
  • Are heading into something significant: growth, raise, sale, audit
  • Are willing to invest in the right financial foundation

Bonzer is probably not for you if you:

  • Are a sole trader or very early-stage start-up
  • Just need a bookkeeper or tax accountant (we know good ones we can refer)
  • Are looking for the cheapest option (we are not it)
  • Are not ready to change anything about how you currently run finance

FAQ

Questions people always ask

Everything you might be wondering, answered up front.

What size businesses do you typically work with? +
Bonzer works best with businesses between $1M and $20M in revenue, typically with 5 to 50 employees. Below that, a good bookkeeper is usually all you need. Above that, a full-time CFO often makes more sense.
How is this different from my existing accountant or bookkeeper? +
Your bookkeeper records what happened. Your accountant handles tax and compliance. Bonzer is the layer above both, helping you understand what the numbers mean, plan what to do next, and make better decisions. We work alongside your existing team, not instead of them.
Do I have to commit to an ongoing partnership upfront? +
No. The 30-day fix engagement stands on its own. Many clients want to start there to see how we work together. If you decide a partnership makes sense afterwards, the minimum is three months, then month-by-month.
How long does a typical engagement last? +
The 30-day fix is exactly that, 30 days. Ongoing partnerships have a three-month minimum but most clients stay for 12 months or longer. You are never locked in beyond what we agreed.
Do you work in person or remotely? +
Almost everything is delivered remotely as standard, using video calls, secure file sharing, and your existing accounting systems. For clients in Toronto or Sydney, in-person meetings can be arranged. Most clients find remote works better than they expected.
What if I'm not sure I'm a good fit? +
That is exactly what the free 30-minute call is for. If Bonzer is not the right answer for your business, I will tell you, and where I can, I will point you to someone who is.
Why a Chartered Accountant for this kind of work? +
The CA designation means rigorous training, real qualifications, and a professional standard. For finance leadership at this level, you want someone who has been properly trained, not just experienced. Bonzer brings both.
What does the first call involve? +
A 30-minute video call. We talk about your business, what is working, what is not, and what is causing you the most stress right now. You leave with a clearer picture of your situation. No pitch, no obligation, no follow-up sales sequence.
